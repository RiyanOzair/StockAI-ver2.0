STOCKAI v2.0 - RESEARCH REFERENCES
==================================

The following research papers were used as primary references for the development of StockAI's multi-agent architecture and market simulation kernel. Full PDFs for the most recent and relevant papers are included in this folder.

INCLUDED PDFs:
--------------
1. When AI Meets Finance: Exploring the Potentials of Large Language Models in Stock Market Simulation (StockAgent)
   - Authors: Mingyu Jin, et al. (2024)
   - Filename: StockAgent_2024.pdf

2. TradingGPT: Multi-Agent System with Layered Memory and Distinct Characters for Enhanced Financial Trading Performance
   - Authors: Yang Li, et al. (2023)
   - Filename: TradingGPT_2023.pdf

3. BloombergGPT: A Large Language Model for Finance
   - Authors: Shijie Wu, et al. (2023)
   - Filename: BloombergGPT_2023.pdf

4. FinGPT: Open-Source Financial Large Language Models
   - Authors: Hongyang Yang, et al. (2023)
   - Filename: FinGPT_2023.pdf

ADDITIONAL LITERATURE SURVEY REFERENCES:
----------------------------------------
5. MarketGym: RL Environment for Financial Markets
   - Authors: Chen et al. (2022)
   - Note: Focuses on Reinforcement Learning substrates for quant trading.

6. Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning for Liquidation Strategy Analysis
   - Authors: Xiao-Yang Liu et al. (2021)
   - Note: Explores optimal execution and market-making strategies.

7. Stock Market Prediction Using Machine Learning: A Systematic Review
   - Authors: Ahmed et al. (2018)
   - Note: A comprehensive benchmark of LSTM and SVM models for time-series forecasting.

8. Intelligent Agents in Market Simulations
   - Authors: Garcia et al. (2009)
   - Note: Early foundational work on autonomous agent behaviors in synthetic environments.
